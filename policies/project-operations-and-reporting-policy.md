# PROJECT OPERATIONS AND REPORTING POLICY

*restore280 Institute — Adopted by the Board of Directors by Written Consent, effective October 1, 2026*

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs operational reporting, deviation escalation, and Board oversight for all Projects. It applies uniformly to every Project regardless of type, size, or cost; this policy provides no exemption based on a Project's scale.

This policy governs reporting, escalation, and oversight only. It does not govern the evaluation, ethical, or execution-specific standards that apply to a Project by virtue of its type; those standards are set forth in the Corporation's other companion policies under Bylaws Section 10.2.

---

## 2. Definitions

**Project.** Has the meaning assigned to Project in the Strategic Prioritization and Resource Allocation Policy.

**Baseline Record.** The purpose, scope, current goals, and timeframe for a Project, as documented under Section 3.

**Material Deviation.** A change to a Project's approved purpose, scope, or resourcing significant enough that the Board's original approval of the Project no longer reflects what is actually being done.

---

## 3. Baseline Documentation at Approval

The proposal presented to the Board for approval of a Project under the Strategic Prioritization and Resource Allocation Policy shall include, for each Option proposed for Project-level selection:

(a) the purpose and scope of the Option;

(b) its current goals; and

(c) the timeframe for achieving those goals.

Where another companion policy already requires a Project to maintain records equivalent to the Baseline Record, that documentation satisfies this Section and need not be duplicated.

---

## 4. Quarterly Progress Reporting

A human designated by the Executive Director shall prepare a written progress report covering all active Projects and present it to the Board concurrently with the quarterly financial summary required under Bylaws Section 8.4. The progress report is a standalone document. It is not part of the quarterly financial summary and is not subject to the Treasurer's accuracy review under Bylaws Section 8.4.

For each active Project, the progress report shall include:

(a) current status measured against the Baseline Record;

(b) any Material Deviation identified during the quarter and its resolution or current status; and

(c) any other material development affecting the Project.

---

## 5. Material Deviation Escalation

A Material Deviation shall be flagged to the Board as soon as it is identified, rather than held for the next quarterly progress report.

A Material Deviation requires the Board's affirmative vote to allow the deviating change to continue. Pending that vote, the deviating change may not proceed. The Project otherwise continues under its last Board-approved Baseline Record.

---

## 6. Amendment

This policy may be amended by majority Board action pursuant to Article VII of the Bylaws.
