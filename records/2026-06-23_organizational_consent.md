## Preamble

The undersigned majority of the then-serving directors of restore280 Institute, a Delaware nonprofit corporation (the "Corporation"), acting pursuant to Article VII of the Bylaws and Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby consent in writing to the adoption of the following resolutions without a meeting. This consent has the same force and effect as a unanimous vote taken at a duly convened meeting of the Board of Directors.

**Electronic Signature Mechanism.** Pursuant to Section 7.1 of the Bylaws and consistent with the mechanism established in the Corporation's IP Transfer Agreement dated June 23, 2026, each director's approval of the pull request introducing this document into the governance repository at `https://github.com/restore280/governance` constitutes a valid electronic signature by that director, with the same legal effect as a handwritten signature. The timestamp and username recorded by GitHub for each approval constitute the date and identity of the signature. This consent is fully executed when all then-serving directors identified in the signature block below have approved the pull request.

## Resolution 9: Spending Thresholds

**9(a) Dual Authorization Threshold.** Pursuant to Section 8.2 of the Bylaws, withdrawals or disbursements in excess of **$1,000** shall require dual authorization by two of: the Board Chair, Treasurer, or Executive Director. For so long as the Executive Director and Treasurer offices are held by the same individual, this dual authorization requirement shall be satisfied by the signatures of the Board Chair and the Executive Director/Treasurer.

**9(b) Unbudgeted Expenditure Threshold.** Pursuant to Section 8.3 of the Bylaws, the Executive Director is authorized to approve unbudgeted expenditures below **$5,000** without separate Board approval. Expenditures at or above that threshold require Board approval pursuant to Article VII.

## Resolution 17: Executive Director Compensation

*This resolution is adopted by the Independent Directors of the Corporation acting without the Executive Director present, pursuant to Section 8.7 of the Bylaws. Enik Nadir Linden did not participate in, deliberate on, or consent to this resolution. Only the signatures of Bridger Ryan Farnsworth and Rachel B. Pickerel are required for and constitute the adoption of this resolution.*

The Independent Directors, having reviewed the compensation practices of comparable nonprofit organizations and having considered the Corporation's current pre-revenue stage of operations, hereby determine that the Executive Director shall receive no salary or other compensation for service in that capacity for the period commencing June 23, 2026 and continuing until the Independent Directors make a subsequent compensation determination. The basis for this determination is that the Corporation has not yet commenced revenue-generating operations and no salary is practicable at this stage. This determination shall be revisited by the Independent Directors no later than the first annual governance review following the commencement of material organizational operations.

The Independent Directors confirm that this determination was made without the Executive Director present and constitutes the advance approval required by Section 8.7 of the Bylaws and the rebuttable presumption procedure under Section 4958 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Resolution 18: Adoption of Bylaws Amendments

Pursuant to Article XIII of the Bylaws, the Board hereby adopts the following amendments to the Bylaws as ratified in Resolution 1, effective retroactively to the date of incorporation, June 23, 2026:

(a) new Section 1.5, Mission and Governing Principles;

(b) new Section 3.12, Mission Stewardship and Director Standards;

(c) amendment of Section 7.3 to establish a minimum synchronous meeting frequency;

(d) amendment of Section 7.5 to include mission-alignment and succession-plan review in the annual governance review;

(e) new Sections 9.6, 9.7, and 9.8, addressing Mission Integrity and Program Alignment Review, Organizational Independence, and Succession and Continuity Planning respectively;

(f) amendment of Section 13.2 to add a limit preventing elimination or material weakening of Section 9.7;

(g) amendment of Section 14.2 to strengthen the dissolution asset distribution standard;

(h) new Section 14.4, requiring written findings before any merger or fundamental restructuring, and the corresponding retitling of Article XIV; and

(i) amendment of various administrative provisions throughout the Bylaws to permit the Executive Director to delegate specified clerical and administrative duties to a designated member of the Corporation's team, without altering any duty involving signature authority, spending authority, or the Executive Director's personal accountability to the Board.

The full text of these amendments as adopted is reflected in the version of BYLAWS.md filed in the governance repository as part of the pull request introducing this consent. No amendment adopted by this Resolution violates the limits of Section 13.2.
